# My Favorite Series

**Title:** *Iron Man Series (Marvel Cinematic Universe)*  
**Genre:** Action / Science Fiction / Superhero  

## Why I Like It
- The story of Tony Stark shows how intelligence, courage, and heart can change the world.  
- The armor suits and technology are amazing.  
- The movies mix action, humor, and emotion.  
- Iron Man’s journey teaches responsibility and sacrifice.  

## Favorite Characters
1. **Tony Stark (Iron Man)** — a genius inventor with a brave heart.  
2. **Pepper Potts** — loyal, caring, and strong.  
3. **James Rhodes (War Machine)** — Tony’s best friend and fellow hero.  

## Memorable Quote
> “I am Iron Man.” — Tony Stark  

## Fun Facts
- The first *Iron Man* movie (2008) started the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).  
- Tony built more than 80 versions of the Iron Man armor in the movies.
